# Stocker Locale Glossary (Approved Terminology)

| English Term | Hindi Translation (हिंदी) | Marathi Translation (मराठी) | Usage & Context |
|---|---|---|---|
| Store | दुकान (Dukaan) | दुकान (Dukaan) | Vendor's business establishment |
| Vendor | दुकानदार (Dukaandar) | दुकानदार (Dukaandar) | The merchant / store owner user |
| Bill | बिल (Bill) | बिल (Bill) | Sale document / receipt |
| Credit (udhaar) | उधार (Udhaar) | उधार (Udhaar) | Customer credit / account |
| Payment | भुगतान (Bhugtan) | जमा / देयक (Jama) | Cash or UPI payment received |
| Balance | बकाया (Baqaya) | बाकी (Baki) | Remaining balance owed |
| Stock | स्टॉक (Stock) | स्टॉक / मालसाठा (Stock) | Inventory on hand |
| Product | सामान / उत्पाद (Samaan) | वस्तू / उत्पादन (Vastu) | Item sold in store |
| Category | श्रेणी (Shreni) | वर्ग / श्रेणी (Shreni) | Product classification |
| Supplier | सप्लायर (Supplier) | पुरवठादार (Puravathadar) | Goods distributor / vendor |
| Reorder | पुनः आर्डर (Reorder) | पुनर्नोंदणी (Reorder) | Low stock restock order |
| Expiry | समाप्ति तिथि (Expiry) | मुदत संपण्याची तारीख (Expiry) | Product expiration date |
| Void | रद्द करें (Radd karein) | रद्द करा (Radd kara) | Reverse a completed sale |
| Deactivate | निष्क्रिय करें (Nishkriya karein) | निष्क्रिय करा (Nishkriya kara) | Soft-delete a record |
| Quantity | मात्रा (Matra) | प्रमाण / संख्या (Praman) | Item count or volume |
| Unit | इकाई (Ikai) | एकक (Ekak) | Measurement unit (kg, piece, etc.) |
| Total | कुल (Kul) | एकूण (Ekun) | Total amount |
| Paid | प्राप्त (Prapt) | दिलेले / प्राप्त (Prapt) | Amount paid at billing |
| Due | बकाया (Baqaya) | बाकी (Baki) | Amount due |
