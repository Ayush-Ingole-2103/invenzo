Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 October 2026

1. Introduction

INVENZO is a multi-store inventory and billing platform designed for independent retail stores. This policy explains what information is collected, how it is handled, and how your store data is isolated and protected.

2. Information Collected

When you operate a store on INVENZO, the application stores:

  • Store & User Information: Store name, owner name, contact phone, email address, physical address, city, and hashed sign-in credentials.
  • Inventory and Billing Data: Products, categories, suppliers, stock movements, and completed sale bills.
  • Customer Credit (Udhaar) Records: Customer names, 10-digit mobile phone numbers, and transaction balances recorded solely for keeping customer credit accounts with their explicit agreement.
  • UPI Identification: The store UPI ID entered in Settings is used only to generate on-screen QR codes for billing and payments.

3. What Platform Developers Can and Cannot See

INVENZO enforces strict tenant isolation:

  • Developers can see: Store profile details, active product counts, total billed revenue, and aggregate sales counts per week, month, and year.
  • Developers CANNOT see: Product names, individual SKUs, prices, customer names or phone numbers, individual bills, customer credit ledgers, stock movements, or store UPI IDs.

4. Third-Party Sharing and Messages

  • WhatsApp: Payment reminders and bill sharing open WhatsApp on the user's own device with prefilled text. INVENZO does not transmit customer data to WhatsApp or external servers.
  • Payments: INVENZO does not process, receive, or hold customer payments. Payment collection is handled directly between the store owner and customer via cash or UPI.

5. Data Retention and Erasure

Store owners can deactivate products, categories, suppliers, and customers. A customer whose balance is zero can have their personal details erased from the system at any time.

6. Contact

For questions or privacy requests regarding your store's records, please contact your store platform administrator.
